Certificates & standards

We operate to international quality and environmental-management standards. Certificate copies, scope and validity, and declarations of conformity are provided on request.

ISO 9001:2015
Quality management

Certified quality-management system across development, production and confectioning.

ISO 14001:2015
Environmental management

Environmental-management system covering energy, waste and recycled-fibre programs.

SATRA
Material testing

Independent testing of footwear and leather-goods materials.

DEKRA EXAM
ATEX · antistatic media

Certificate for antistatic AGT-BT filter media used in ATEX-classified zones.

EU 1935/2004
Food contact · with 10/2011

Declarations of conformity for materials and belts in food-contact applications.

REACH
Substances (EC 1907/2006)

Declarations of conformity on substances of very high concern, on request.

ISO 16890
Air-filter classification

Air-filtration media classified to ISO 16890 (ePM1 / ePM2.5 / ePM10 / Coarse), which replaced EN 779.

OEKO-TEX
Textile safety

Harmful-substance testing for applicable textile products; scope on request.
